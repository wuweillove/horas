Horas privacy policy

Horas is a freelancer time desk. This policy covers the site and the Android app com.horas.app.

Data stored on your device

Hours, breaks, comments, jobs, clients, rates, invoice drafts, and settings stay in this browser or on the phone. Horas does not upload them.

If you sign in with Google, Horas receives your Google account id, name, and email and keeps them on the device. Horas does not receive your Google password.

A backup file is created only when you download one. Restoring a backup reads a file you choose.

Ads on Android

Advertising runs only in the Android app, and only after the Google consent form says ads may be requested. If you do not consent, the app does not request ads.

When ads are allowed, Google AdMob may process the advertising identifier, IP address, device and app identifiers, ad diagnostics, and your consent choice. Horas does not sell hours, client names, invoice contents, or comments.

Google describes what AdMob collects at policies.google.com/privacy and support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543.

Permissions

The Android app declares internet (Google sign-in and ads), notifications and a foreground service so a running clock is not frozen, plus the advertising permissions merged by the Google Mobile Ads SDK. It does not request location, contacts, camera, microphone, or SMS.

Children

Horas is a work tool. It is not directed at children under 13.
